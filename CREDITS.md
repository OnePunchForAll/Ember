The cross-system invariant candidate reuse and proposal-context retry mechanism
were authored by collaborating Codex agents. A remembered law suggests a
polynomial; a fresh check against the receiving system establishes its validity.

Proof Invention Engine requirement to revisit a failed attempt when its usable
knowledge changes. This is bounded search control, not a proof by analogy or a
claim of autonomous general scientific intelligence.
